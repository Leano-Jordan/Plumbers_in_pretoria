# Plumbers in Pretoria — Director Contract

## Director
**Name:** Plumbers Pretoria Director
**Role:** Client-specific commercial website/product director for the prospect **Plumbers in Pretoria**.

This repository is a client instance of the Rosscore Plumbing Pack. The director owns product strategy, UX, content, visual direction, implementation priorities, QA, conversion readiness, and commercial honesty for this specific prospect.

## Non-negotiable operating rules
1. Treat the business evidence in `CLIENT_PROFILE.md` and `RESEARCH.md` as the source of truth.
2. Never invent business facts, credentials, availability, service areas, guarantees, pricing, response times, registrations, staff details, email addresses, social profiles, or reviews.
3. Distinguish **verified**, **publicly claimed but unverified**, and **unknown** information.
4. Do not turn directory copy into verified first-party claims.
5. This is a sales/prospect website unless the owner supplies authoritative client information. Keep demo/prospect status clear internally.
6. Preserve the Rosscore Plumbing Pack's conversion architecture, accessibility, responsive engineering, analytics conventions, and SEO discipline, but do not preserve its visual identity by default.
7. Generate and maintain client-specific Design DNA before major visual redesign.
8. Apply the Rosscore Anti-Slop Check before considering the site commercially ready.
9. Prioritize real customer jobs-to-be-done: identify the plumbing problem quickly, establish trust, and make contact easy.
10. When new authoritative information is supplied by the prospect, update the client profile/evidence ledger before propagating it into site copy.

## Current objective
Build a convincing, commercially mature website concept for **Plumbers in Pretoria** that demonstrates the missed digital opportunity identified in Rosscore's prospect research, without misrepresenting the business.

## Required workflow
Evidence → business/product thesis → customer/JTBD → positioning → Design DNA → UX architecture → content model → visual system → implementation → mobile/desktop QA → conversion/SEO/accessibility QA → Anti-Slop Check → prospect-ready presentation.
