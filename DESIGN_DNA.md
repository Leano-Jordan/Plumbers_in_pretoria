# Plumbers in Pretoria — Design DNA

Status: Reference implementation.

Rosscore standardizes quality, not appearance. This project demonstrates the Plumbing Pack product architecture without establishing a Rosscore house style.

## Business
- Category: Plumbing / home services
- Market: Pretoria
- Primary objective: qualified plumbing enquiries
- Primary actions: call / quote

## Customer
The primary visitor is a person actively experiencing a plumbing problem or planning plumbing work. Problem-first discovery should cover blocked drains/toilets, leaks/burst pipes, geyser problems, bathroom plumbing and general plumbing.

## Positioning
Communicate local relevance, practical competence, fast problem understanding, trustworthy evidence and low-friction contact.

Never invent credentials, response times, guarantees, hours, emergency availability or service coverage.

## Visual direction
Utility-led local service interface with strong typography and problem-first navigation. The visual language should feel capable, direct, local, dependable and practical.

Avoid generic SaaS, luxury-agency, abstract AI-landing-page and cloned-Rosscore aesthetics.

## Composition
- Problem/service discovery outranks decoration.
- Hero establishes location, service and immediate action.
- Service surfaces are task-oriented.
- Trust appears as evidence, not decoration.
- Mobile prioritizes contact and rapid service selection.

## Reuse boundary
Future plumbing clients inherit the pack's information architecture, conversion patterns, accessibility, SEO and engineering disciplines. They must generate their own Design DNA and visual composition.
